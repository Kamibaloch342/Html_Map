Strategic Architecture

10. The ILO 5R Policy Framework & 2026–2030 Roadmap

Operationalizing Recognize, Reduce, Redistribute, Reward, and Represent to unlock Pakistan's care economy.

1. RECOGNIZE

Institutionalize annual LFS Time-Use modules by PBS. Establish official Care Satellite Accounts in National GDP. Formally register domestic care workers under provincial labor acts.

2. REDUCE

Scale rural solar-piped water schemes to eliminate 2.7h/wk water fetching. Launch clean-cooking LPG vouchers to eliminate biomass drudgery. Expand rural solar food refrigeration.

3. REDISTRIBUTE

Mandate 5,000 community crèches co-located with BISP payment centers and primary schools. Introduce subsidized paternal care incentives and public eldercare day-centers.

4. REWARD

Legally enforce the PKR 40,700 statutory minimum wage for domestic workers and Lady Health Workers (LHWs). Provide caregiver social security, maternity stipends, and health cards.

5. REPRESENT

Grant collective bargaining rights to Home-Based Care Worker unions. Establish elected BISP Beneficiary Councils for grievance redressal and tripartite wage board advocacy.
